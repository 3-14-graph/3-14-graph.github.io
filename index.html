π-Graph

A map of the people in your life: who they are, how they know each other, what you have done together, and the dates that matter to them.

Yours alone

No account, no server, no analytics. Your graph lives on your own devices and is never sent to us.

Phone and computer

They sync directly over your Wi-Fi, encrypted with a key only the two of them hold.

Notes, if you like

Speak or type what happened. An AI service you pick, with your own key, turns it into changes you approve.
