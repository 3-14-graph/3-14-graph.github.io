Support

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea for making π-Graph better? Write to 3.14graph@gmail.com. It helps to say which device you use and what you expected to happen.

π-Graph has no account and no server, so we can't see your graph. Please don't send it unless you want to; a description is usually enough.

Security problems

If you find a way to get at someone's graph, their keys, or the sync between their devices, please write to the same address with “Security” in the subject, and give us a chance to fix it before you publish the details.

Common questions

My phone doesn't sync with my computer

Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi, with the computer awake and π-Graph open on it. On the computer, open Sync from the π menu, turn on “Let my other devices sync with this computer” and choose “Pair a phone…”, then scan the code with the phone. The iPhone's Camera app opens it, or use “Scan code…” in the phone's own Sync. On Windows, allow π-Graph through the firewall the first time, and make sure your Wi-Fi is set as a Private network.

How do I keep a copy of my graph?

π-Graph takes a backup on its own when you open it, at most once a day and only if something changed. Settings › Data lists them, and “Back up now” takes another. “Save a copy…” writes a file you keep wherever you like. On a phone, backups are deleted along with the app, so save a copy before you remove it.

Why can't I add more people or sheets?

This first version holds up to 100 people and five sheets of your own. Everyone already in your graph stays, and you can still change, export and back up all of it. Room for more is coming; if you need it, tell us at the address above. The π menu's “Coming next” lists what else is on the way.
